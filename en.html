About Liangcheng International

Open, Professional &
Pragmatic — A Cross-Border Platform

Incorporated in Hong Kong, Liangcheng International operates with the full benefit of a free economic system — policy flexibility, unrestricted capital movement, and seamless international connectivity. Free from conventional industry licensing constraints, we integrate resources and coordinate cross-border operations with agility.

Our core team brings together seasoned professionals with extensive European market experience and deep Asian market insight. Well-versed in international business conventions and multicultural environments, we excel at navigating complex cross-border projects and high-profile reception arrangements.

We serve major corporations, industry associations, and high-net-worth individuals, delivering bespoke, end-to-end solutions. With Hong Kong as our fulcrum, we bridge European and Asian resources — a trusted partner for international expansion, cultural exchange, and commercial value creation.
