ABOUT LIANGCHENG

An Integrated Platform for Global Commerce and Curated Travel

Liangcheng International Limited was established in Hong Kong in 2022. Utilizing Hong Kong’s connectivity as a global commercial hub, we have built a cross-border service network spanning Europe and Asia. We focus on resource integration and project synergy, linking premium global partners to ensure the stable implementation of multinational operations.

Our core team has been deeply rooted in the Sino-European markets for many years, possessing profound multinational business backgrounds and cultural perspectives. We are committed to providing outstanding cross-border commercial matchmaking and premium travel resource support.
