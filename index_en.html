ABOUT LIANGCHENG

An Integrated Platform for Cross-Border Resources & Bespoke Travel

Liangcheng International Limited was incorporated in Hong Kong in 2022. Utilizing Hong Kong's connectivity as an international trade hub, we have built a cross-border service network spanning Europe and Asia. Our core mission focuses on resource integration and project synergy, flexibly connecting premium global partners to support the stable implementation of various multinational business types.

Our core team has been deeply involved in the Sino-European market for many years, possessing profound multinational business backgrounds and cultural insights. We are committed to providing outstanding cross-border business matchmaking and curated travel resource support for various enterprises, industry associations, and high-net-worth individuals.
